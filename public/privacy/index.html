Privacy Policy — Webs

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Webs ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Webs mobile application. Webs is a private messaging, presence, and location-sharing app operated by Emigidius Kashoju from Tanzania. By using Webs, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Name — the display name you provide at registration.
  • Phone number — used to verify your account and match you with contacts who are also on Webs.
  • Email address — used only for account verification and password recovery.
  • Profile photo (optional) — a photo you choose to upload.

1.2 Messaging Data

  • Text messages you send to other Webs users. These are stored on our server (at webzapp.org) so they can be delivered even if the recipient is offline. Messages are retained on the server until both sender and recipient acknowledge receipt.
  • Read and delivery receipts — metadata indicating whether a message has been delivered and read.
  • Typing indicators — short-lived signals showing that you are currently typing in a conversation.

1.3 Contacts

  • Phone contacts — with your explicit permission, Webs reads the phone numbers in your contact list to determine which of your contacts also use Webs. Contact data is sent to our server as a salted hash for matching; your contacts' names, email addresses, and other details are never transmitted or stored on our servers.
  • You can revoke contact access at any time from your phone's Settings → Apps → Webs → Permissions.

1.4 Location Data

  • Precise location — collected only while you have an accepted location-sharing request with another user, or while you are using the "Find My Device" feature on your own account. Location is transmitted to our server and visible only to the specific user you are sharing with.
  • Location sharing ends immediately when either party ends the request, and no further location is transmitted after that point.
  • You can revoke location permission at any time from your phone's Settings → Apps → Webs → Permissions. Revoking permission prevents any future transmission.

1.5 Device Information

  • Device model, operating system, and app version — collected so we can detect crashes and push app updates to you.
  • Push notification token (FCM) — a Google-generated identifier used to deliver message notifications. You can disable notifications from Settings → Apps → Webs → Notifications.
  • IP address — logged by our web server whenever your device communicates with it. Retained for up to 90 days for security and abuse-prevention purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To deliver messages between you and other Webs users.
  • To let other users know whether you are online or typing.
  • To fulfill location-sharing requests that you have explicitly accepted.
  • To enable the "Find My Device" feature for your own registered devices.
  • To send push notifications about new messages, calls, or location requests.
  • To detect abuse and prevent spam or fraud.
  • To notify you of app updates that fix bugs or add features.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties. We do not read the content of your messages for any purpose other than delivering them.

3. Sharing of Information

3.1 With Other Users

Your name, profile photo, online status, and typing indicators are visible to users you exchange messages with. Your location is visible only to users who have an active, accepted location-sharing request with you.

3.2 Service Providers

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — receives a push token and message metadata (not message content) to deliver notifications.
  • DigitalOcean — hosts our backend server (located in Frankfurt, Germany). Your data is stored on their infrastructure.
  • Mapbox / OpenStreetMap — processes map tile requests when you view the map. They receive map viewport coordinates but not your identity.
  • OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine) — public routing service that receives the start and end coordinates when you request a route, to compute the path between them.

3.3 Legal Requests

We will only disclose your data when required by valid legal process, and we will notify you of any such request unless legally prohibited from doing so.

4. Data Retention

  • Account data — retained until you delete your account.
  • Messages — deleted from our server once both parties have received them, or after 90 days, whichever comes first.
  • Location history — deleted automatically 30 days after a location-sharing request ends.
  • Web server logs — retained for 90 days.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for contact, location, or notification access at any time via your phone's Settings.
  • Export a copy of your messages and profile data.

To exercise any of these rights, email emigidiuskashoju@gmail.com. We respond to all requests within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Webs is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

All data transmitted between your device and our server is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher). Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. Access tokens are stored using the operating system's secure storage.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, email us immediately at emigidiuskashoju@gmail.com.

8. International Transfers

Your data is stored on servers in Germany (DigitalOcean Frankfurt). By using Webs, you consent to this transfer. We comply with applicable data protection laws including the GDPR where applicable.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will: change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and notify you via in-app message or push notification if the changes are significant.
